Discovery Engine

Pipeline: find → identify → verify provenance → verify rights → deduplicate → moderate → publish. Nothing enters the public collection automatically.

  1. 01Source connectors (Civitai, Hugging Face, RSS)
  2. 02Ingestion & metadata normalization
  3. 03Provenance check (A/B/C, C2PA evidence)
  4. 04Rights check (rights_basis required)
  5. 05Duplicate detection (SHA-256, pHash)
  6. 06Human moderation
  7. 07Publication (verified only)

Status: CLI pipeline operational (discover → verify → moderate). Publication remains manual; no auto-publication.

Rights & Offer

WAAM does not claim copyright over published images. Every artwork carries a rights status and a rights basis. We respect an explicit author opt-out even when the formal license would permit display.
