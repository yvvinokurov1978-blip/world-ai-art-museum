# WAAM Terms of Use (Offer)

Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-11-04
Project: World AI Art Museum (WAAM)
Status: Non-commercial research project

## 1. About the project

World AI Art Museum (WAAM) is a non-commercial research platform that collects, verifies, and publishes AI-generated artworks for study, documentation, and comparative research. WAAM is not a commercial gallery and does not sell artworks.

## 2. Use of materials

Materials published on WAAM (images, passports, metadata) are provided for:
- research and study;
- personal, non-commercial use;
- citation with attribution to WAAM and to the original author/source.

Commercial extraction, resale, or redistribution of images as standalone works is not permitted without a separate agreement with the rights holder.

## 3. Copyright

WAAM does not claim copyright over published images. Each artwork retains the status stated in its passport (`rights_status`, `rights_basis`).

Where the source jurisdiction does not grant copyright to pure AI-generated output, WAAM treats the image as research material and publishes it with full attribution.

## 4. Author submissions

By submitting an artwork, the author confirms that:
- they have the right to submit it;
- they grant WAAM a non-exclusive, non-commercial license to display it for research purposes;
- they understand that WAAM does not commercialize the image and does not claim copyright.

## 5. Takedown and corrections

Any author or rights holder may request correction or removal:
- Contact: [yvvinokurov1978@gmail.com]
- Response time: within 72 hours
- Removed works are moved to the "Negative collection" (ID + reason, no image shown)
- All decisions are logged

WAAM respects an explicit author opt-out even when the formal license would permit display.

## 6. Future commercialization

If WAAM introduces commercial elements in the future (e.g., advertising), this will be a separate layer and will not change:
- the rights status of already published works;
- the provenance levels;
- the non-exclusive license granted by authors.

Any commercial use of a specific image would require a separate agreement with its author.
